About Weddings by Harsha

Crafting Heartfelt Celebrations with Passion, Precision & Heritage

Weddings by Harsha is a premier event management and wedding production house headquartered in Bengaluru. From the first spark of conceptualization to the final farewell of your last guest, we create immersive experiences so you can immerse yourself in joy.

Founded and led by Harsha, our team brings together seasoned art directors, master floral craftsmen, sound engineers, and hospitality veterans. Whether it’s an intimate traditional ceremony at a heritage kalyana mantapa in Jayanagar, a lavish destination wedding in royal Mysuru, or a high-tech corporate convocation for institutions like KIMS, we orchestrate every nuance with uncompromising excellence.

Our Own Office & Godown Advantage

Located near 3rd Main Rd, Pipeline West, Kasthuriba Nagar, Azad Nagar, Bengaluru 560026, our sprawling warehouse houses our own inventory of aluminum trusses, stage fabrication, ambient lighting fixtures, exotic brass vessels, and floral accessories. This ensures superior quality control, custom craftsmanship, and unmatched pricing for our clients.
