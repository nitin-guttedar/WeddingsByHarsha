About Weddings by Harsha

Crafting Heartfelt Celebrations with Passion, Precision & Heritage

Weddings by Harsha is a premier event management and wedding production house headquartered in Bengaluru. From the first spark of conceptualization to the final farewell of your last guest, we create immersive experiences so you can immerse yourself in joy.

Founded and led by Harsha, our team brings together seasoned art directors, master floral craftsmen, sound engineers, and hospitality veterans. Whether it’s an intimate traditional ceremony at a heritage kalyana mantapa in Jayanagar, a lavish destination wedding in royal Mysuru, or a high-tech corporate convocation for institutions like KIMS, we orchestrate every nuance with uncompromising excellence.

Free Guide & Checklist

Bengaluru & Mysuru Wedding Planning Checklist

Essential timeline milestones curated by Harsha for a smooth, joyful experience:

1

9–12 Months Before: Foundational Decisions

  • Finalize wedding dates & muhurtham timings with family astrologer
  • Establish overall budget and priority allocation (mandap, feast, venue)
  • Consult Weddings by Harsha for venue shortlisting and godown inventory preview
  • Lock venue contract (Kalyana Mantapa / Resort / Palace lawn)
2

6–9 Months Before: Aesthetics & Core Vendors

  • Finalize mandap theme (traditional banana leaf/sugarcane vs modern floral pastel)
  • Book catering partners and conduct South/North Indian food tastings
  • Confirm photography & 4K cinematic film team
  • Design bridal entry and sangeet stage lighting/truss setup
3

3–6 Months Before: Attire, Invitations & Hospitality

  • Finalize Kanjeevaram silks, sherwanis, and jewelry
  • Order physical wedding cards & digital WhatsApp wedding invitations
  • Audit hotel room blocks for outstation relatives in Bengaluru / Mysuru
  • Map guest transit, airport/railway station pickups and tempo travellers
4

30 Days to D-Day: Flawless Execution

  • Final walk-through at venue with Harsha & production coordinators
  • Finalize pooja samagri checklist and fresh temple flower orders
  • Schedule sound checks, DJ playlist, and anchor scripts
  • Relax and enjoy! Weddings by Harsha team takes over all logistics on ground.
